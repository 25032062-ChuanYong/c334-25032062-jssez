03 / Incentives and tax watch-outs

Check eligibility and plan early.

The playbook describes incentive packages and facilitation support. Eligibility depends on the applicable requirements.

Headline incentives in the playbook overview

5% corporate tax for 10 years or 15 years for companies with capital investment excluding land of at least RM500 million, and RM1 billion in the manufacturing sector, for qualifying activities.

5% personal income tax for knowledge workers.

Sector-specific incentives, grants and facilitation support are also described.

Plan around the requirements

  • Structure activities carefully and manage tax exposure, transfer pricing and regulatory compliance.
  • Align banking arrangements with incentive reporting and audit requirements.
  • Engage the Invest Malaysia Facilitation Centre-Johor early for eligibility and application guidance.
  • Check customs and trade compliance for cross-border operations.

04 / Passes for staff

Make staff mobility part of the plan.

The playbook's appendix describes several routes. Check the right pass and current requirements through the official channels before making arrangements.

Employment Pass (EP)

For expatriate posts assessed by MIDA in manufacturing, selected services and approved regional establishments. Applications use the Xpats Gateway System. The playbook lists salary categories and notes revised minimum salary requirements effective 1 June 2026.

Professional Visit Pass (PVP)

A temporary route for professional work such as training, consultancy or project work, including machinery installation. The playbook describes a stay of up to 12 months and says it is not extendable.

Investor Pass

For eligible foreign business travellers, including new, pipeline and existing investors. The playbook describes stays of up to 12 months, in periods of six months, with an extension subject to requirements.

Prepare your workforce

Plan cross-border team design, targeted upskilling and local leadership development. The playbook identifies Enterprise Singapore, the Johor Talent Development Council and MIDA as training support channels. Pass details: SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, Appendix, pages 28–29.
